RESOLVED (2026-08-13): GPIO18 was previously wired as a fake "GND" driven
LOW by firmware (gpio_set_level), which left the TB6612's logic ground
floating until the ESP32 booted and caused the power-sequencing fault
where the motor only ran if USB power came up before the battery. The
wire has been moved to a real GND pin on the board; the GND define and
all its gpio_config/gpio_set_level uses have been removed from
Motor_Drive_With_IDF/main/main.c.

OPEN (T5 task): GPIO 3 and GPIO 46 (enAB, enBA) are ESP32-S3 strapping
pins — sampled at reset to configure boot behaviour. Not yet verified
whether the encoder's idle/active levels on these pins are safe across a
reset. Confirm against the ESP32-S3 datasheet's strapping-pin table
before treating this wiring as final; fallback is moving the encoder to
free GPIOs.
