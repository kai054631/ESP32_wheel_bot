Near-term goal — the robot runs

An 8-tutorial drive-first track (60–90 min each): get the robot driving open-loop as a 2-wheel differential robot first, then make it accurate with encoders and closed-loop control. IMU/GY-85 work and full pose fusion are deferred until after that.

Stage 1 goal (after the drive-first track)

The robot maintains and reports a continuously updated estimate of its own pose — (x, y, θ) — fused from wheel-encoder odometry and the GY-85 IMU.
"It knows where it is" means it can print/log this estimate in real time.

Full syllabus (after the robot runs)

  1. A
    I2C bus & GY-85 raw comms
    I2C scan, register-level reads from ADXL345 / ITG3205 / HMC5883L.
  2. B
    Sensor calibration & units
    Counts → g / °/s / heading, gyro bias, hard/soft-iron basics.
  3. C
    IMU-based heading
    Gyro integration, complementary/Kalman fusion with magnetometer heading.
  4. D
    Sensor fusion for pose
    Combine encoder (x, y) with IMU-corrected heading into one pose estimate.
  5. E
    Stretch: logging / visualization / EKF
    Plot the driven path, discuss a proper EKF framing.
